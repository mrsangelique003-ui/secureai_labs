Scope & Focus Areas

What we publish

We accept high-quality original research across six core areas of AI security and digital safety.

🛡️

AI Security & Adversarial ML

Attacks against AI systems, adversarial examples, model robustness, prompt injection and defences for machine learning pipelines.

🔒

Privacy-Preserving AI

Federated learning, differential privacy, homomorphic encryption and techniques enabling collaborative AI without exposing sensitive data.

⚖️

AI Ethics & Fairness

Bias detection, algorithmic fairness, accountability, transparency and the social impact of AI in high-stakes domains.

🌐

Network & Infrastructure Security

AI-driven threat intelligence, intrusion detection, zero-trust architecture and securing critical infrastructure.

🧬

AI in Healthcare Security

Privacy-preserving medical AI, securing health data ecosystems and safe deployment of clinical AI systems.

📜

Cybersecurity Policy & Law

Digital sovereignty, international AI governance frameworks, cyber law and policy for emerging technologies.

For Authors

Submit to the JSCI Journal

We welcome original research, review articles, case studies and position papers from academics, practitioners and students worldwide. All submissions undergo double-blind peer review. Publication is free until December 2026.

01
Prepare Your Manuscript

Format using our Word template. Max 12,000 words for full papers; 4,000 for short communications.

02
Submit by Email

Send to journal@secureailabs.org with your cover letter, keywords and institution.

03
Peer Review (4–6 weeks)

Double-blind review by domain experts. Constructive feedback provided regardless of outcome.

04
Publication & Indexing

Accepted papers published open-access with ISSN, DOI and Google Scholar indexing — at no cost.
