Programme Benefits

More than just work experience

🎯

Dedicated Mentorship

Every intern is paired with a senior industry mentor who provides weekly 1-on-1 coaching, career guidance and technical support throughout the placement.

📄

Verified Credentials

Receive a verified digital credential and detailed performance report from SecureAI Labs — independently verifiable by any employer worldwide.

🌍

Global Network Access

Join our growing network of alumni and partner organisations. Our community actively supports members with opportunities and connections.

💰

Stipend Support

All placements include a monthly stipend. For students facing financial barriers, additional hardship grants are available through our community impact fund.

🎓

Continued Learning

During your internship, access all SecureAI Labs courses and weekly live skills workshops — completely free for active interns throughout their placement.

📈

Career Placement

Our career team works with every intern to help them secure their next opportunity after the placement ends.
