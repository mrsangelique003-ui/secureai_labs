VIDEO CHALLENGE — Community Submissions
=========================================
Save participant videos in this folder with these filenames:

  challenge1.mp4   — Participant 1 video
  challenge2.mp4   — Participant 2 video
  challenge3.mp4   — Participant 3 video

Supported formats: .mp4 (recommended), .webm
Aspect ratio: 9:16 (vertical / portrait) works best

The videos appear automatically on games.html in the Video Challenge section.
To add more slots, duplicate a video slot div in games.html.
